OUR HERITAGE

Since 2026

A Legacy of Quiet Luxury

Founded in the heart of India by renowned couturiers Umesh Sharma, Soham Setia and Madhav Gupta, Maison Éclat was born from a simple belief: true elegance is never loud.

For over a century, we have preserved the art of handcraftsmanship — from the ateliers of Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré to our flagship maison in Chandigarh, India.
